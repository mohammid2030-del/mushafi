# Night palette — 3.0.52 (102)

Night mode now uses midnight charcoal/petrol (#0c161c), blue-petrol surfaces (#162830), warm ivory (#f0ece3), readable muted text (#b4c2c3) and champagne accents (#d7bc86). Glass cards, navigation, selected states, controls, inputs, sheets, playback surfaces, Quran highlighting and loading screens share the palette. The Islamic pattern is more subdued in night mode; Quran pages remain plain. All new stylesheet rules are scoped to body.dark. No dimensions, reader content, account/sync data, audio controls or animation timings were changed.

The separate startup HTML includes the same night stylesheet and initial dark background. Native LaunchScreen fallback colors, MainActivity dark surface/system-bar backgrounds and LiveBroadcastView dark container/text colors match. Every light branch retains its original literal colors; launcher resources and the public live-stream wrapper are unchanged.

Validation compares all visible light-mode computed foreground/background colors, gradients, borders, shadows, fill, accent and color-scheme across 11 representative screens with the new stylesheet enabled versus blocked. They are identical. Night contrast against the card surface: primary text 12.90:1, muted text 8.29:1, gold 8.28:1; primary-button text also exceeds 4.5:1. Navigation, reader, adhkar, learn, khatma, prayer settings, reciter list and playback screens were checked for horizontal overflow. Startup dark palette and the plain Quran canvas are checked. Browser checks do not claim physical-device or video-provider restyling tests.
